## Product Principles

1. **Lead with Production Impact:** Highlight Kubernetes, distributed systems, and telemetry upfront; hiring managers look for proven infrastructure ownership.
2. **Clear Technical Taxonomy:** Group technical competencies logically into backend, cloud/platform, databases, and observability instead of an unstructured bullet list.
3. **Show Career Trajectory:** Prominently display the progression from intern to full-time backend engineer to platform engineer at Pagesoft.
4. **Zero Dead Ends:** Every listed project or link must be functional, eliminate placeholder links, and provide a direct path to download the updated resume and initiate contact.
